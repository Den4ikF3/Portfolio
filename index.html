Dodge Landing Page

WEBSITE / LANDING / AUTOMOTIVE

VISIT SITE

About

Development of a conceptual landing page for a legendary automotive brand. The main goal was to convey the aggressive character and unbridled power of Dodge through the visual style of the web interface.

I used a dark theme with neon accents to emphasize the sporty spirit. Special attention was paid to adaptability (Responsive Design) - the site adapts perfectly to any device, preserving the dynamics of the composition. Implemented hover effects and smooth animations that immerse the user in the atmosphere of speed.
